FB On Facebook, WIA content continues to lead, accounting for the majority of reach, views, and link clicks. Personality-led content remains the primary driver on this platform.
LI On LinkedIn, product-focused posts are generating clicks on their own. The two top-clicking posts were product proof points rather than personality features, and non-WIA content accounted for most LinkedIn clicks.

④ What July is telling us

Priority Improve Facebook reach. The strong engagement rate reflects a smaller reach base. Since posting activity was steady, this points to a distribution factor to investigate. In the meantime, WIA content remains the most effective format on Facebook.
Opportunity Scale the LinkedIn product-focused format. These posts are generating clicks without a named expert, which reduces reliance on the WIA campaign and is worth expanding.
Direction Overall: maintain WIA as the engagement and Facebook-conversion driver, while growing the LinkedIn product-focused format that converts on its own.
To monitor WIA campaign contribution has been easing month-over-month, and sharing activity was light in July. Both are worth keeping an eye on as amplification levers.

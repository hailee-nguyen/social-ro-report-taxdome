The story The strongest awareness month of 2026 on LinkedIn and the second strongest on Facebook, and Women in Accounting is the reason. WIA content delivered 86% of Facebook reach and every Facebook share, and a carousel repurposed from WIA became the month's biggest LinkedIn post. Both engagement rates eased, but absolute engagement grew on both platforms: a denominator effect, not weaker content.
Watch DASR pre-event content is underperforming with the summit on October 22: 8 of 12 DASR posts on Facebook earned zero reactions, comments or shares, and pain-point posts were the weakest format on both platforms.
Conversion Landing-page clicks held at 160 (−4%), the second-best month since March. Reach grew about 46 to 50% without lifting LP traffic, so visit to reach fell on both platforms. Non-WIA clicks kept growing (68 → 76), easing WIA's share of clicks from 59% to 53%.

② The funnel · TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

TOFU LinkedIn impressions reached 4,556 (+50% MoM, nearly 10× YoY), the highest of 2026. Two drivers: more posts (25 vs 18) and the repurposed WIA carousel on client relationships (Laura Camelia Ștefan, Florina Mateescu, Cornelia Nastase), which drew 1,372 impressions, about 35% of the month's post-level total. The baseline rose too: excluding each month's top post, LinkedIn posts averaged about 108 impressions vs about 84 in August.
TOFU Facebook reach grew to 4,484 (+46% MoM, nearly 14× YoY), second only to April. All of the growth came from WIA: non-WIA posts averaged about 43 reach each, unchanged from August, while WIA reach rose from 2,566 to 3,841. Three of four WIA profiles broke out above 700 reach (Ioana Dănilă, Lianda Munteanu, Mihaela Diaconu), against two of five in August; together they delivered 69% of Facebook reach.
TOFU Facebook views reached 12,033 (+11% MoM). Views grew far less than reach, so views per person reached fell from about 3.5 to 2.7: September reached a wider, newer audience rather than re-reaching the same people.
YoY Year-over-year growth is mostly a volume effect: September 2025 had one LinkedIn post and two Facebook posts, against 25 and 23 this year. Per post, this year's averages (about 159 LinkedIn impressions, 195 Facebook reach) sit below last September's single customer testimonial video (Răzvan Macsim). Customer testimonial video was almost absent this month.

Facebook views · monthly
Mar–Sep 2026 · total organic views per month

Views reached 12,033 in September, the second-highest month of the year; the three WIA profiles contributed about two thirds.

MOFU LinkedIn engagement rate eased to 4.34% (from 5.93%), but likes, comments and reposts rose from 130 to 156 (+20%): impressions grew faster than engagement. The month's top post shows "rates lie" clearly: the client relationships carousel drew 729 clicks (74% of all LinkedIn post clicks) yet reads a below-average 2.77% ER, because LinkedIn's rate excludes clicks.
MOFU Facebook engagement rate eased to 6.20% (from 6.77%), while reactions, comments and shares rose from 63 to 113 (+79%). WIA posts produced 79% of those interactions and all 10 Facebook shares; across both platforms, 13 of 16 shares came from WIA content. YoY, ER is up about 2.5pp on both platforms.
BOFU Landing-page clicks held at 160 (−4% MoM) after August's rebound. The issue is efficiency: Facebook visit to reach fell from 4.88% to 3.19%, LinkedIn from 0.56% to 0.35%. WIA LP clicks fell 15% (99 → 84) even as WIA reach grew: the Aug 3 profile turned 1,161 reach into 49 link clicks, the Sep 21 profile turned 1,223 reach into 17. Comparing CTA and link placement on those two posts would show why.
BOFU The healthier signal: non-WIA LP clicks grew for a third month (36 → 68 → 76), and WIA's share eased from ~59% to ~53%. The destination of non-WIA traffic isn't broken out; if most of it is the DASR registration page, DASR content converts better than its engagement suggests.

DASR The 12 DASR posts on Facebook delivered 12% of reach and 22 interactions, 12 of them on Alina Goldenberg's card. Pain-point posts were the weakest format on both platforms: zero Facebook engagement and 6 LinkedIn engagements across 6 posts. Speaker intros did far better on LinkedIn (65 engagements) than on Facebook, and the best performer on both is a known local face, consistent with the Romania pattern of named local experts carrying engagement.
Also weak The three HR webinar follow-ups (Alina Dragoș quote, 10 HR risks demo CTA, ITM video) reached 91 people on Facebook with 2 interactions. In all, 11 of 23 Facebook posts earned zero reactions, comments or shares.
To scale Repurposed WIA carousels on LinkedIn produced the month's biggest post and highest clicks, for the second month running (Aug 13: 250 clicks; Sep 17: 729). A DASR-angled version, with WIA voices on the topics DASR will cover, would bridge both tracks.
To fix Click yield on WIA profiles. Reach is growing but WIA LP traffic fell 15%. Compare the Aug 3 and Sep 21 posts to find the difference in CTA or link placement.

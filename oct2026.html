② The funnel · TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

TOFU Facebook views reached 15,557 in 20 days (+29% vs September's full month, +94% on daily pace). Meta's own period view shows views up 135% and viewers up 148% vs the previous 20 days. The Simona Rusu profile alone earned 3,754 views and 1,462 reach, the highest single-post Facebook reach since April.
TOFU Facebook reach is at 3,882, 13% below September with ten days to go and ahead on daily pace. Year-over-year it is a step change (545 in Oct 2025, +612%): per post, reach went from about 42 to about 259.
TOFU LinkedIn impressions are at 2,571 (−15% on daily pace). Per-post performance held: about 100 impressions per post, against about 108 in September once September's breakout WIA carousel (1,372 impressions) is excluded. The gap comes from that breakout being absent. Year-over-year, impressions per post are flat on a post-export basis (1,621 → 1,599).

Facebook views · monthly
Mar–Oct 2026 · total organic views per month (October = Oct 1–20)

Views reached 15,557 in the first 20 days of October, already the highest month of the year.

MOFU Facebook engagement rate rose to 20.69% (from 6.20%). The tracker's Facebook rate counts all clicks, and October had 657 Facebook clicks against 165 in September. Of those, 550 came from the two WIA profiles: 434 were non-link taps (such as See more or profile taps) and 62 were photo clicks. On interactions alone, ER is 3.76% vs 2.52%. On the same basis as October 2025 (interactions plus link clicks), it is 5.74% vs 2.02% (+3.72pp).
MOFU LinkedIn engagement rate rose to 5.07% (from 4.34%) while absolute engagement fell: likes, comments and reposts dropped from 156 to 69 (−34% on daily pace). This is a denominator effect, with the higher rate sitting on a smaller impressions base. Shares and reposts held at 16 (+33% YoY), 9 of them on the WIA Facebook posts.
BOFU Landing-page clicks reached 106 in 20 days, level with September on daily pace (−0.6%). Facebook delivered 90 and LinkedIn 12. Facebook visit to reach fell to 2.32% (from 3.19%) because the extra clicks on the WIA posts were mostly not link clicks. LinkedIn visit to reach rose to 0.47% (from 0.35%).
BOFU WIA's share of landing-page clicks eased to 49% (from 53%). WIA delivered 52 clicks (pace −7%), recovering from the July low of 28. Non-WIA content delivered 54 and is pacing slightly ahead of September (+7%), its fourth month of growth. The two WIA profiles converted differently: Simona Rusu turned 1,462 reach into 22 link clicks (1.5%), Ancuța Necula turned 640 reach into 32 (5.0%).

FB On Facebook, the three WIA posts carried the period: 2,491 of 3,882 reach (64%), 5,991 of 8,535 post views (70%), 57 of 77 link clicks (74%) and 9 of 15 shares. WIA remains the strongest campaign on this platform.
FB The Alin Vlad speaker card was the most-reacted post of the period, with 53 reactions against 28 on the top WIA post. Non-WIA posts also lifted: about 116 reach per post vs about 47 in September, or about 73 without Alin Vlad.
LI On LinkedIn, two DASR topic carousels framed a summit session and ended with the registration link. Together they earned 49 of 89 post clicks (55%) from 104 impressions. Their ER reads 1.8% and 4.1% only because LinkedIn's rate excludes clicks: the "rates lie" effect. WIA on LinkedIn drew engagement (20 of 69 engaged actions) but only 3 clicks.
Pattern The split from earlier months is now consistent: WIA wins Facebook, carousels win LinkedIn clicks. The carousel worked in September with the WIA client-relationships carousel and again in October with the DASR topic carousels, so the format converts regardless of which campaign feeds it.

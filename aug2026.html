The story A strong distribution month across the board. Facebook views, reach, and LinkedIn impressions all grew, led by two widely shared Women in Accounting features and a clear recovery in sharing activity (16 shares/reposts, up from 1). Facebook's engagement rate eased to 6.77%, but this reflects a much larger reach base, not weaker engagement — absolute engagement roughly doubled for the month.
LinkedIn LinkedIn is the standout: impressions and engagement rate both rose, led by the DASR 2026 speaker announcement (1,047 impressions) and a new repurposed-WIA carousel that drew exceptional clicks. Because the rate improved while the audience also grew, engagement increased faster than reach — the healthiest combination.
Conversion Landing-page clicks rebounded to 167 (+161%), the first increase after several months of gradual decline. Facebook drove most of it. The watch-item: WIA's share of clicks rose again to ~59%, so the month leaned on a small number of high-performing posts.

② The funnel — TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

TOFU LinkedIn impressions grew to 3,040 (+54% MoM, and close to 4× year-over-year), supported by renewed sharing (16 shares/reposts, up from 1) and — the single biggest contributor — the DASR 2026 speaker announcement for Dr. Nataliia Vovchuk, which drew 1,047 impressions on its own, roughly a third of the month's LinkedIn total. The DASR pre-event content is starting to build reach ahead of the October summit.
TOFU Facebook reach recovered to 3,073 (from 788, and +413% year-over-year), lifted mainly by two Women in Accounting features that were shared into the featured experts' networks. This is the named-expert amplification pattern working as intended.
TOFU Facebook views reached 10,859 (+94% MoM, the second-highest month of the year). Two WIA posts (~3.3K and ~2.5K views) contributed just over half of all views. Reach and views also came from a broader, less concentrated audience this month — about 3.5 views per person reached, versus roughly 7 in July.

Views recovered to 10,859 in August, the second-highest month of the year, with the two WIA features contributing just over half.

MOFU LinkedIn engagement rate rose to 5.93% (from 3.57%) while impressions also grew — meaning engagement increased faster than the audience. Estimated engaged actions moved from ~71 to ~180. A higher rate on a larger audience makes LinkedIn the healthiest platform this month. Two posts show the split cleanly: the DASR speaker announcement lifted the rate (6.49% on high impressions), while the repurposed WIA carousel drove clicks the rate doesn't count (250 clicks on 258 impressions, yet a below-average 4.26% ER) — the "rates lie" effect, since LinkedIn's rate excludes clicks and so understates the platform's full contribution.
MOFU Facebook engagement rate eased to 6.77% (from 12.82%). This is a denominator effect: reach grew much faster than engagement, so the rate came down even though absolute engagement roughly doubled (~101 → ~208 estimated actions). July's higher rate sat on a smaller reach base, so the two months aren't directly comparable — the year-over-year view (3.01% → 6.77%) is the cleaner read, and it is up.
BOFU Landing-page clicks rebounded to 167 (+161% MoM), the first increase after several months of gradual decline, taking the funnel back above its March level. Facebook led the recovery (LP-from-FB 52 → 150), consistent with WIA content driving clicks on that platform.
BOFU WIA's share of landing-page clicks moved back up to ~59% (from 44%), as the two high-reach features drove much of the month's traffic. The independent base still grew in absolute terms (non-WIA 36 → 68), so the momentum is genuine — WIA simply grew faster.

FB On Facebook, the two lead WIA profile features carried the month, together contributing just over half of all views and most of the sharing. Standard personality-led WIA content remains the primary reach and amplification driver on this platform.
LI On LinkedIn the drivers are different, and notably neither is a standard WIA profile. The DASR 2026 speaker announcement (Dr. Nataliia Vovchuk) drove the impressions at 1,047 — the top LI post of the month and about a third of its total impressions — and its 6.49% ER lifted the blended rate. This is the DASR pre-event content beginning to build distribution ahead of the October summit.
New format The second LinkedIn standout is a repurposed WIA carousel — a new initiative that reuses Women in Accounting interview material as thought-leadership / educational content. Early results are strong: 250 clicks on 258 impressions (~97% click-to-impression), converting on the strength of the message and five experts rather than a single named face. Its ER reads a modest 4.26% only because the rate excludes those clicks — the value is real. A format worth scaling.
To watch A pattern is forming across both platforms: expert-backed but not single-personality formats — the Facebook 3-expert carousel and this LinkedIn 5-expert repurposed carousel — are earning their place in the top posts. Developing these broadens the base of consistent performers beyond individual profile features.
